QUICK STARTNew here? How to fish
1 · Cast & wait

Choose Day/Night or Clear/Rain if you like, then select CAST LINE. Watch the bobber and wait for a bite.

2 · Hook the bite

When a bite appears, use HOLD TO REEL to set the hook. Then read the fish's on-screen intent.

3 · Match the fight

Tension: hold REEL to gain line and release to ease pressure. Patience: tap TAP IN RHYTHM; do not hold REEL. Follow the cue and meters.

4 · Keep or sell

After a catch, choose SELL CATCH for Essence or KEEP CATCH to add the specimen to your collection.
